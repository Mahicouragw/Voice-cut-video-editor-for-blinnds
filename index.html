Workflow

  1. Upload video
  2. Play, stop at 30s, add Music 1 at 30% volume
  3. Add Music 2 at 60%, move, trim
  4. Record Voice-over at 100%, enable medium noise reduction
  5. Enable ducking, play result, split video, undo split, export

Audio Mixing

Never automatically merge audio clips during editing. Each track has independent volume, mute, position, trim, fade, noise reduction until final export.
