Privacy

Videos, recordings, and voice-over files are private user content. Processing happens locally using Web Audio API, HTML5 Video, and Canvas capture. No video is uploaded to a server without your consent. We do not sell or share user media. Export uses local MediaRecorder.

Local processing: Trimming, splitting, volume, fades, noise reduction, ducking, preview playback.

Optional cloud AI: automatic captions and AI noise removal can process audio on the secure VoiceCut service. Nothing is uploaded without your explicit approval for each request. Temporary server copies expire within 15 minutes.

Terms of Use

VoiceCut Studio is provided for personal video editing. You are responsible for the content you edit and share: only edit media you own or have permission to use. Cloud AI features are rate-limited per device to keep the shared service fair; excessive automated use may be blocked. No warranty is given; review AI captions and enhanced audio before publishing.

    Help and Accessibility

    VoiceCut Studio - Accessible Editing

    This editor is designed from the beginning for accessibility. Every important control has a meaningful accessible label and screen-reader announcement.

    TalkBack (Android) Focus Order

    1. Project name
    2. Video preview
    3. Playback controls
    4. Current position
    5. Timeline tracks
    6. Selected clip controls
    7. Add audio
    8. Add voice-over
    9. Audio controls
    10. Video controls
    11. Undo/Redo
    12. Export

    TalkBack never becomes trapped inside the timeline. Use "Exit timeline editing" button.

    VoiceOver (iOS)

    Correct accessibility labels, traits, slider values, logical swipe navigation, no duplicate elements, no inaccessible custom canvas.

    Workflow

    1. Upload video
    2. Play, stop at 30s, add Music 1 at 30% volume
    3. Add Music 2 at 60%, move, trim
    4. Record Voice-over at 100%, enable medium noise reduction
    5. Enable ducking, play result, split video, undo split, export

    Audio Mixing

    Never automatically merge audio clips during editing. Each track has independent volume, mute, position, trim, fade, noise reduction until final export.
